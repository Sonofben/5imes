The visitor book at reception has been there for decades. It is cheap and everyone understands it. It also has problems most offices only notice when something goes wrong.

## The problems with a paper register

### Everyone can read everyone

Each visitor writes their name, phone number and who they came to see, on a page the next visitor can read. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, personal data should be kept secure and only seen by people who need it.

### Nobody knows who is still inside

A book records arrivals, rarely departures. During a fire drill or an emergency, reception cannot say who is still in the building.

### Hosts are not told

Reception phones around to find the host, or the visitor waits. Busy receptions lose track.

### Records are kept forever

Old books sit in a cupboard for years. The law expects you to keep personal data no longer than necessary.

## What a digital visitor system should do

1. **Let visitors sign in on their own phone**, so no shared pen and no open book.
2. **Keep reception in control**, so nobody walks in until reception admits them.
3. **Tell the host** the moment their visitor arrives.
4. **Show who is on site**, staff and visitors together, for safety.
5. **Delete records automatically** after a period you choose.
6. **Show a privacy notice** that visitors accept before signing in.
